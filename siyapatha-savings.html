    FAQs

    Good questions, clear answers.

    Which savings accounts does Siyapatha offer?

    Abhiman (senior citizens), Kirula (children), Siyapatha Saver (general savings) and Siyapatha Plus (progressive savings).

    Is there an account for children?

    Yes. Kirula is the Children’s Savings account for those up to 18 years.

    Is there an account for senior citizens?

    Yes. Abhiman is the Senior Citizen Savings account for those aged 55 and above.

    What is the annual effective interest rate?

    It is the yearly return on your savings after interest is compounded, so it is slightly higher than the stated interest rate.

    How can I access my money?

    Through our islandwide branch network and the islandwide LankaPay ATM network.

    Will I be notified of transactions?

    Yes. You receive real-time SMS notifications for all transactions.
