Factoring

Factoring is a working capital solution specially designed for businesses and organizations to continue business operations on credit terms. Our working capital solutions are a fast way to collect money for the products delivered and services offered. At Siyapatha Finance, we ensure that you increase or maintain a positive cash flow within your business during challenging times.

  • Working capital
  • Same-day liquidity

Fixed Deposits

Siyapatha Finance Deposits are guaranteed assurance of Stability, Service, and Benefits for your hard-earned money; the wind beneath your wings for you and your dreams. Be it an emergency, local or foreign tours, children’s higher education, purchasing of your new house, property, or your dream apartment, Siyapatha Finance Deposits will enable you to invest your money and obtain a return on the capital to fulfil your dreams.
